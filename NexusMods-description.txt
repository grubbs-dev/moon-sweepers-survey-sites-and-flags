[size=5][b]Survey Sites and Flags 0.1.4[/b][/size]

Shows all flags and survey sites on the station Moon Map, even before they have been discovered.

[b]0.1.4 fixes[/b]
[list]
[*]Fresh saves now show undiscovered flags and survey sites.
[*]Map markers use the live globe view scale for accurate placement.
[*]Undiscovered markers are gray; discovered locations are high-contrast red.
[*]Avoids duplicate native markers.
[/list]

[b]Installation[/b]
Extract the archive into the Moon Sweepers folder containing MoonSweepers.exe. The DLL must be located at:

BepInEx/plugins/SurveySitesAndFlags/SurveySitesAndFlags.dll
