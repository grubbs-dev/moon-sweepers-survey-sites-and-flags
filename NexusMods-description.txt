SURVEY SITES AND FLAGS

Survey Sites and Flags adds every survey site and flag to the orange Moon Map inside stations, making it easy to see which locations are still missing.

FEATURES

- All flags and survey sites are shown on the station Moon Map.
- Markers use the game's actual world coordinates and line up with their real locations on the globe.
- Undiscovered flags and sites appear gray.
- Untangled flags and surveyed sites appear bright green.
- Native discovered markers are hidden to prevent duplicate icons.
- No extra HUD, no F9 menu, and no changed controls.

HOW TO USE

1. Walk to the orange Moon Map console inside any station.
2. Press E to open the Moon Map.
3. Gray markers are undiscovered locations.
4. Green markers are flags you have untangled or sites you have surveyed.

REQUIREMENTS

- Moon Sweepers 1.0.5 (Windows x64)
- BepInEx 6 IL2CPP win-x64
- The Moon Sweepers BepInEx patcher/bootstrap used by your installation

BepInEx download:
https://github.com/BepInEx/BepInEx/releases

INSTALLATION

1. Close Moon Sweepers.
2. Extract the archive into the folder containing MoonSweepers.exe.
3. Confirm the file exists at BepInEx\plugins\SurveySitesAndFlags\SurveySitesAndFlags.dll.
4. Launch the game and open the station Moon Map.

UNINSTALLATION

Remove BepInEx\plugins\SurveySitesAndFlags.

MULTIPLAYER

The mod reads the game's existing landmark and discovered-state data. For best results, use the same mod version for every player. Multiplayer behavior follows the game's normal shared world-state synchronization.

COMPATIBILITY

Built and tested against Moon Sweepers 1.0.5. The mod is focused on the station Moon Map and does not alter the standard M map.
